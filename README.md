# 🎬 My Favorite Movie: *Inception*

**Inception** is a science fiction thriller directed by *Christopher Nolan*.  
It explores the concept of **dreams within dreams** and questions what is real.

---

## 🧠 Main Characters
- **Dom Cobb** — A skilled thief who steals secrets from people’s dreams  
- **Arthur** — Cobb’s partner and organizer  
- **Ariadne** — The talented architect of dreams  
- **Eames** — The forger who can change appearances  

---

### 🔍 Favorite Quote
> “You mustn’t be afraid to dream a little bigger, darling.” — *Eames*

---

### 🎯 Why I Love It
1. The **mind-bending** concept of dream layers  
2. The *emotional depth* of Cobb’s story  
3. The brilliant **visual effects** and **music** by Hans Zimmer
